M4 screens · cross-project dashboard

The dashboard is the first rail item and the page you land on. It has an action queue on top (In review, Mentions, Stalled, Deadlines), one row per project, and the agents. The design follows the Ledger direction and adds no new colour tokens; component styles are in ledger-m4.css. Every frame is shown in light and dark, 1280 wide unless marked 1024.

Layout rules

  • The queue is the only boxed surface on the page, with a 2px top rule in the text colour. Projects and agents sit on the page ground, so the queue reads first without an accent colour.
  • All queue rows share one grid: glyph, key, title, project, who, priority, and a right-aligned reason. Columns line up across groups.
  • Shape tells the groups apart: square, @, half-filled square, diamond. Overdue and stalled always carry words in a pill ("no update 26h", "11 days overdue"), never colour alone.
  • Mentions take two lines: who, the task, then the excerpt with the @ chip.
  • With the panel open, rows drop to key, title and reason. Project rows drop to key, name, open and active.

Calls I made on the brief

  1. The rail count sits under the "Dashboard" label, like the counts under project keys, not as a corner badge. The Ledger rail has one width, so there is no collapsed form to draw.
  2. The rail count is always the Mine count, even while the page shows Everyone.
  3. An empty group stays as one quiet line ("Stalled 0 · Nothing stalled") instead of disappearing. The layout doesn't jump on refresh, and keys 1 to 4 always land on the same group. When all four are empty, the queue becomes a single all-clear line.
  4. Deadlines under Mine are milestones and cycles where you or your agents hold an open task.
  5. A background refresh never moves rows under keyboard focus. A row whose condition cleared stays until focus leaves it.
  6. The panel opened from the queue scrolls to the newest comment. M focuses the reply box, so a mention can be answered without leaving the page.
  7. The scope is kept in the URL (?scope=everyone).

Open questions

  1. Project rows: I capped them at 12 with "Show all 30" (frame 9b). The agreed scope says every project. Keep the cap, or list them all?
  2. Mentions show "Agent asks" or "Person asks" in the who column. Is that useful, or should the column stay empty?
  3. New on the dashboard opens quick create with no project chosen, so the project picker gets focus first. OK?
  4. The Backlog.md tag on project rows is a placeholder until M3 settles its marker.
