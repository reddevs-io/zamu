1Three directions for the task list with the task panel open, light and dark

Context

Self-hosted project management for solo devs and small teams. AI agents are members and change tasks next to humans, so every change shows who made it and, for agents, which human they act for.

This screen is where people spend most of their time, so it carries the visual decision. The other eight screens follow the pick in round 2.

Shared across all three

  • Same structure: sidebar, top bar, filter row, grouped list, side panel.
  • Agents look different from humans without a badge: a square or diamond avatar, or a ◆ mark.
  • Keyboard hints in place: C new task, / search, Esc close, J/K move.
  • Long titles truncate in the list and wrap in the panel (PAY-140).

Assumptions

  • Statuses: Backlog, Todo, In progress, In review, Done, Canceled.
  • Priorities: None, Low, Medium, High, Urgent.
  • Comments ship later; the panel reserves the composer.
  • Fonts are open-license Google Fonts so the app can bundle them.
