Payouts that fail at the provider are dropped today. Retry them on a schedule so merchants are paid without support stepping in.

  • Back off from 1 minute up to 6 hours, with jitter.
  • Stop after 8 attempts and alert the on-call channel.
  • Every attempt reuses the payout’s idempotency key, so a retry can never pay twice. See payouts/retry.ts.

Schedule

Attempt n waits min(6h, 1m × 2^(n−1)) plus up to 20% jitter. Eight attempts cover about 13 hours, which is long enough to ride out a provider outage overnight and short enough that a merchant notices before the next business day.

attempt  wait      cumulative
1        1m        1m
2        2m        3m
3        4m        7m
4        8m        15m
5        16m       31m
6        32m       1h 03m
7        1h 04m    2h 07m
8        6h        8h 07m

Out of scope

  • Retrying refunds. They go through a different provider endpoint and need their own idempotency rules (PAY-160).
  • A merchant-facing retry button. Support can trigger a retry from the admin panel instead.
  • Changing the alert routing. On-call keeps the existing #payments-alerts channel.

Open questions

Should a payout that fails with account_closed retry at all? It never succeeds, so the current plan skips straight to the alert. Tomás wants a single retry in case the provider reports it wrongly, which happened twice in September.
